Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This website is a design concept for a fictional private country club. The club, its history, its prices, and its membership details are invented placeholders for a demo — nothing here is real.

Plain version

This site collects nothing from you. It is a single static page with no forms, no accounts, no cookies, and no analytics. There is nowhere to enter your information and nothing tracking your visit.

What we collect

Nothing directly. The only data anyone touches is at the hosting layer:

  • Hosting logs: this site is served by a static hosting platform (Cloudflare Pages), which may keep standard server logs — IP address, pages visited — for security purposes. We never see or use them.

How we use it

There is nothing to use. We don't sell, rent, or share information about you, because we never had any.

Your choices

Because this is a concept demo, there is no data controller and no mailbox to write to about your information. If a real club ever used this design, this page would name exactly who holds your details and how to reach them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date.
